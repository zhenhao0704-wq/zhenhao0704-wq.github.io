About

Zhenhao Wen is a performer, researcher, photographer, and educator.

He is a Ph.D. student in Performance Studies at the University of Hawaii at Manoa. His research examines how bodies produce knowledge through movement, improvisation, image-making, and teaching.

His creative work focuses on bodies that are ordinary, unfinished, or overlooked, and on how performance and images can keep their experience visible.

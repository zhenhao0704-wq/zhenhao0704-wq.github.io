EPIC

30 September–4 October 2026

Performer · Dance Theatre

I perform in EPIC, a dance-theatre production directed by Pei-Ling Kao at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Created with Sunny Nai-Hsuan Yang and the cast, the work brings different dance forms and bodies together through movement.

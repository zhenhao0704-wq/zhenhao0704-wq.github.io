Profile

Performance Studies PhD student, dancer and choreographer trained at Shanghai Theatre Academy and the University of Edinburgh. My work connects improvisation, bodily awareness, emotional archives and trans-Pacific dance circulation with teaching and dance filmmaking.

Performance and choreography include the male lead Dahe in Yellow River, dance choreography and performance for the feature film 1921, and touring work in the UK, France and South Korea. Allan Wilson’s four-star review of Elegies singled out “the particularly sinuous Edwin Wen” (All Edinburgh Theatre, 2023).

Oil Tank Culture Park · Seoul

ARTSinTANK Dance Festival in Korea

28 June to 3 July 2026 · Performed

International MDD Collaboration

The ninth ARTSinTANK Dance Festival in Korea ran at Oil Tank Culture Park in Seoul from 28 June to 3 July 2026. Its MDD programme brought musicians, digital artists and dancers from six countries and regions into one residency. I danced in its 3 July showcase at 5:00 p.m. in Tank 2.
